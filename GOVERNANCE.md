# Governance

## Roles

- **Lead maintainer:** Natalie Boedecker. Sets direction, makes final decisions, cuts releases, and manages repository settings.
- **Maintainers:** trusted contributors whom the lead maintainer appoints. They review and merge changes. There are none yet.
- **Contributors:** anyone who opens an issue or pull request.

## Decisions

- Most decisions happen in issues and pull requests, by discussion.
- Maintainers aim for agreement. If they can't reach it, the lead maintainer decides.
- Changes to public contracts, versioning, or these rules need the lead maintainer's approval.

## Community plugins

New community plugins aren't accepted until the lead maintainer opens the library to them. Every change in `community-plugins/` needs a lead maintainer's review, because that code runs with the server's full privileges once an admin enables it.

## Conduct

The [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) applies to everyone, including maintainers.
